# Written Consent of the Board of Directors

**OpenEMR Foundation, Inc.**
**November 20, 2026**

The Board of Directors of OpenEMR Foundation, Inc., acting by unanimous
written consent in lieu of a meeting, adopted the following resolutions:

RESOLVED, that the Board adopts the OpenEMR Trademark Usage Policy
substantially in the form presented in pull request #18 to the
foundation-minutes repository, with such non-substantive revisions as
trademark counsel may recommend, to be maintained at
trademark/TRADEMARK_POLICY.md as the authoritative version, and
authorizes the officers to publish it and establish
trademark@open-emr.org;

FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Board approves the logo currently in use as
the Foundation's logo mark and authorizes the officers to engage
trademark counsel to file an application to register it, at a cost not
to exceed $1,000; and

FURTHER RESOLVED, that the officers may take actions reasonably
necessary to implement the foregoing, this consent to be filed with the
minutes.
